# Banned Bets

- Objects not brought on the players person to the session (i.e. finding something in the room that just happened to be there)
- Shoes
- T-shirts
- Blackjacks
- Deodorant
- Limbs/body parts
- Glasses
- Antihistamines & any type of medication (tablets, capsules, etc.)
