# Banned Bets

On special occasions dealer may allow a player to bet non-TTs currency if the rest of the table is in agreement. Dealer say is final on all value exchanges. Value exchanges will only take place at the table during a scheduled session if all other players agree to the exchange. Players should state whether they wish to exchange for chips or for-like payout. 

Dealer will not agree to for-like payout if the dealer cannot complete a for-like payout (e.g. a flatscreen TV)

BETS CAN BE UNALLOWED AT THE TABLE AT THE DEALER'S DISCRETION.

Players cannot bet:

- Objects not brought on the players person to the session (i.e. finding something in the room that just happened to be there)
- Shoes
- T-shirts
- Blackjacks
- Deodorant
- Limbs/body parts
- Glasses
- Antihistamines & any type of medication (tablets, capsules, etc.)
- Expired LCC discount cards
- Political leaflets (intact or damaged)
- Uncooked rice/pasta
- Social events/outsourcing organising
- Counterfeit currencies
- Portraits
- Small animal toys
- Easter chicks
- Sexual favours (apart from between players)
- GBP, USD, RMB, SGD
